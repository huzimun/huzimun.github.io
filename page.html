AI security.
Multimodal and generative models.

I am a Ph.D. student at Tianjin University. My research sits at the intersection of AI security and generative modelling — I study how multimodal large models and text-to-image diffusion models can be attacked, protected, and made safer.

Specifically, I work on adversarial attacks and defenses for diffusion models, deep neural network IP protection through model encryption, and preventing unauthorized identity customization in generated images.

Interested in my research? humingwang@tju.edu.cn
